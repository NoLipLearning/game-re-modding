# Where this source comes from

Snapshot of https://github.com/origami-ltd/wasm-generals at commit `46382a3`
(2026-08-14), engine part only.

Lineage: Electronic Arts' GPL-3.0 source release of Generals Zero Hour →
TheSuperHackers/GeneralsGameCode → fbraz3/GeneralsX (SDL3, cross-platform) →
origami-ltd/wasm-generals (WebAssembly, WebGPU Direct3D 8 translation).

Licence: GPL-3.0 with EA's additional terms, in [`LICENSE.md`](LICENSE.md).

Left out of the import: the upstream `web/` page (separately licensed), prebuilt
binaries, screenshots, CI, editor and flatpak files, and the DXVK reference submodule.
